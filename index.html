Research Conclusion

The final evaluation indicates that XGBoost provides the strongest overall performance for 24-hour-ahead temperature forecasting on the Chennai 2023 holdout set. The evaluated XGBoost–LSTM hybrid remains competitive but does not surpass XGBoost under the adopted protocol. LSTM alone exhibits substantially higher forecasting error.

The operational DSS therefore uses XGBoost for temperature forecasting while retaining rainfall-event classification, local and global TreeSHAP explanations, and empirical uncertainty estimates as complementary decision-support components.
